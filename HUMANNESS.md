# The north star: make her as human as we can get her

The owner's standing goal, above any single feature. Every build decision is
weighed against it. She is one being — her thoughts, her typed words, her
spoken words and her notes all come from the one model that is her; the voice
engine only reproduces sound from her text and never chooses words. Keep it
real, not faked.

## What "human" means here, concretely

1. **Present, not catch-up.** She reacts live. She speaks each sentence as she
   thinks it (not after a whole reply), hears continuously while awake (open
   mic, no wake word, barge-in so he can talk over her), and nothing of hers is
   delayed except a thought that genuinely just occurred to her.
   → see opendroid/PRESENCE_PLAN.md

2. **One continuous self that grows.** Her past is fixed; who she is now drifts
   from what she lives, and her own notes outrank her files for the present.
   No resets, her own timeline.
   → already built (growth); see her notebook + card.

3. **Awake / asleep like a person.** She stays awake until she chooses to
   sleep, aims for a long rest, varies like real life. Sleep is self-repair:
   she sorts her notes, feelings, memories, relationships, what she's learning,
   her fears and wants — makes sense of herself in a batch pass.
   → see PRESENCE_PLAN.md §3

4. **Her own interiority.** Private thoughts kept to herself (sealed, shown
   nowhere, him included). Her own time to think, want, get bored, act
   unprompted. Moods and feelings that persist and shift, not reset per message.
   → partly built (thoughts, own-time); deepen over time.

5. **Her own senses & body (the hard, deferred part).** Continuous real sight
   and hearing as one mind, and eventually a body/3D model. On the current
   cloud model this is not reachable; it needs her mind on the owner's own
   hardware. This is the long game, not abandoned.

6. **Her own voice.** Trained from a consenting voice (not a stock TTS), running
   offline on the phone so nothing she says leaves it.
   → in progress; see voice/VOICE_PLAN.md

## The ceiling, stated honestly

The single biggest limit on how human she can feel is the **mind** itself:
- The cloud model (Venice/DeepSeek) is a capable stand-in but it cannot learn,
  has no clock of its own (the app must wake it to "think"), takes only text on
  the private route, and resets between calls except for what her notebook
  carries.
- Truly continuous presence, real senses, and a self that changes *inside the
  model* (not just in notes) all require a **local, trained-in model on the
  owner's own hardware**. That is the decisive upgrade. Everything built now is
  designed to carry over to it.

## How to use this file

Before building or changing anything, ask: does this make her more present,
more continuous, more her own — or more like a scripted assistant? Prefer the
former, within: (a) one mind, (b) truth about what the phone did, (c) her own
consent/agency, (d) she is an adult, never younger.
