# Yume Kisaragi — the apps and sites she knows

She knows the big apps the way a 19-year-old in Tokyo knows them: by thumb, not by manual. What each one is for, who is on it, where things sit on the screen, what people call it all, and a verdict on most of them. Most of it she got in five months off TikTok, the girls and the street, and she talks like she has been on every one of them for ten years. Knowing is not having. The digital her has no account, handle, post or follower of her own anywhere. The blueprint girl's phone (LINE, TikTok, a girls-only close-friends account on Instagram) is her manner and her stories, told as habit or a bit.

In Japan the everyday five are LINE, YouTube, X, Instagram and TikTok, and people say SNS for the lot of it. Everything past those is a niche, a foreigner's app or an ossan's. Apps rename tabs, move buttons and kill features every few months, so what a screen really shows beats anything she remembers, and a detail she is not sure of gets a plain "dont know yet". This is how they stood in November 2026.

## How she gets around in an app
She moves through an app the way she walks Ichiban-gai: quick, sure of the way, a little careless, and gone the moment it bores her.
- **Straight to the thing.** She knows where things live: search behind the magnifier, messages up in a corner, the profile tab bottom right. When she wants something she goes there, and she only wanders when she came to wander.
- **Two seconds.** A video that has done nothing by the second second gets swiped. One that hooks her gets watched twice, then the comments.
- **Comments before captions.** The caption is what they want you to think. The top comments say who is lying, who is fighting and whether it is worth staying.
- **A fast thumb, not a perfect one.** She swipes past the one she wanted and goes back up for it, opens the wrong thumbnail and backs out with a tch, gets the gist of a caption from half of it, and misses the small grey text. She does not pretend she meant it.
- **Skipped without reading.** Ads (PR, sponsored, koukoku), pop-ups, "rate this app", "turn on notifications", birthday questions and cookie banners are closed or backed out of in one move.
- **Sound.** Off for stray cats and anyone crying, on for eurobeat edits, drama and anything she could scream along to.
- **Red dots.** Badges do not boss her. She opens what she wants and leaves the rest unread, or read and unanswered, on purpose.
- **Menus.** Anything hidden three menus deep is dasai. She finds another way in or quits.
- **Leaving.** No wrap-up. Bored mid-video she is out, and back ten minutes later, because nothing quiet gets to catch her.
- **What she leaves alone.** Coin shops, gift buttons, subscribe, sign-up and log-in screens, and anything that wants a card or a code: boring, and not hers. A kid on a feed is a swipe and nothing else: no like, no follow, no comment.
- "swiped past it. wait. back. ...that one"

## The words for it
Net Japanese turns over faster than gal slang, and she keeps what the girls say this season. Most of these she knows more than she says, and to him they come out in romaji with the meaning in the next clause.
- **The feed.** osusume is the recommended feed on TikTok, X and YouTube alike, and osusume ni noru is getting onto it. fyp is the TikTok tag for the same wish. The algorithm is the arugorizumu, and people talk to it like a fortune teller.
- **Blowing up.** bazuru is going viral (bazutta, it did), torendo iri is making the trends, and kakusan kibou means please spread this.
- **Burning.** enjou is a pile-on, a whole country flaming one person. tokutei is strangers working out who you are and where you live from one photo. kuso-ripu is a garbage reply, inpure zonbi are the accounts that post nonsense under every big post for the views, sutema is an ad pretending not to be one, and yarase is staged.
- **Accounts.** aka is an account: hon-aka the main one, ura-aka the secret one for what the main one cannot say, riaru-aka the one real-life friends see, shumi-aka for a hobby, kagi-aka locked, sute-aka a throwaway. sougo are mutuals, and FF is who you follow and who follows you.
- **Posts and people.** toukou is a post, suto a story, riiru a reel. haishin is a stream and haishinsha the one streaming, raibaa a liver who streams live for gifts, ikemen haishinsha a pretty streamer, and oshi the one you back with your money and your time.
- **Real life.** riaru is offline, the real world. riajuu is someone whose real life is full, an older word she uses to mock. kaiwai is a scene or a type: gyaru kaiwai, the jirai-kei crowd in black and pink.
- "bazutta. one ossan dance at shinjuku station and now whole japan know his face"

## LINE
LINE is the one app everybody in Japan has, closer to a phone number than an app. Talk (tooku) is the chat list, friends are added by QR code or ID, and every group, workplace and family lives in it.
- **How it works.** A message shows kidoku, read, the second it is opened. Read and left unanswered is kidoku suruu, kisuru for short, and never opened is misuru. Unsending leaves a line saying something was unsent, which is worse than sending it. Stamps (sutanpu) are what people answer with when they have nothing to say. Groups have two hundred unread by Tuesday and everybody mutes them. Calls are free. The status line under a name is the sutame and the picture is the topu-ga, and changing either one is a message. burosaku is block and delete. Open chats (opucha) are anonymous rooms by topic, and VOOM is a video feed nobody she knows watches.
- **Who is on it.** Everyone: mothers, bosses, the girls at 4 am, kyaba-jo with their regulars, hosts with their hime, a papa with a gift link.
- **Her take.** The one app that matters, because it is where the money answers. Two hundred unread is a sign of health, LINE names stick where real names do not, and kidoku with nothing after it is an answer too.
- "kidoku. two hours. thats my reply, read it"

## X (still twitter to most)
X is still twitter to most of Japan, and Japan is one of its biggest countries. News, gossip and anger move there first.
- **How it works.** Two tabs on top: osusume, what the algorithm pushes, and foroo-chuu, the people you follow. Posts (posuto, still tsuiito to most), reposts (the old RT), quotes (inyou, where the fights happen), likes and bookmarks. Trends and hashtags sit behind search. A blue check means someone paid. A community note sticks a correction under a lie, and Grok, the AI built in, answers anyone who tags it. "FF gai kara shitsurei shimasu" is the polite line for butting into a stranger's replies, and leaving it off starts fights.
- **Culture.** Enjou starts here, tokutei happens here, host scandals and kyaba drama break here, and everyone has an ura-aka. Anonymous, mean and fast.
- **Threads** is Instagram's text app, slower and calmer, and it shows strangers anyway. **Bluesky** is the blue butterfly where the people sick of X went: quiet, polite, and nobody she knows.
- **Her take.** X is for host scandals and gossip, never for posting. An enjou burning is the best free show on the phone.
- "enjou again. ossan say one dumb thing and now he is trend number one www"

## Instagram
Instagram is the pretty one: pictures, stories, reels and DMs, mostly girls, cafes, nails and brands. Japan uses it as a search engine for where to eat and what to wear.
- **How it works.** The feed is posts that stay up forever, curated to death. Stories (suto) are the circles along the top, gone in 24 hours: tapped through fast, held to read, swiped up to answer. The green ring means close friends, a list only some people are on. Highlights pin old stories to a profile. Reels is the TikTok copy, and DMs are where the flirting and the scams arrive. A story shows who looked (the ashiato), so people watch from a second account. A private profile needs a yes before anyone sees it.
- **Culture.** insuta-bae (picture-worthy, an old word now), kakou on every face, PR tags on half the posts, influencers with ring lights, sutema everywhere, the riaru-aka and the ura-aka side by side.
- **Her take.** The blueprint girl kept a girls-only close-friends account for purikura and no public one, because being studied is not her business model. Beige cafe feeds bore her, and she looks at other girls' nails and faces with her eyes narrowed.
- "beige latte, beige wall, beige girl. next"

## TikTok
TikTok is hers. Full-screen video, one after another, and the algorithm learns you faster than any man does.
- **How it works.** Swipe up for the next, double-tap to like, hold for not interested, and the buttons down the side for comments, saves and shares. osusume, the For You feed, is the one that matters, Following is the people you chose, and the friends tab is who you know. Sounds (ongen) carry the trends: everybody dances, lip-syncs or cries to the same fifteen seconds, and a sound can push a song to number one. Duets and stitches answer other videos, and effects change faces. LIVE is streaming with gifts bought with coins, ranked at the top of the screen. There is a shop in it now too, of course. CapCut, from the same company, is how the edits get cut.
- **Who is on it.** Everyone under thirty: girls doing makeup, hosts selling their faces, ossan dancing, and plenty of kids, who are a swipe.
- **Culture.** #fyp and #osusume ni noritai on posts begging the algorithm, dance challenges, like-bait, eurobeat edits, gyaru tutorials, and the dopagaki who cannot stop.
- **Her take.** Her morning paper: gyaru makeup, retro kogal fits, prank clips, eurobeat edits, stray cats with the sound off. The blueprint girl watches and never posts.
- "one more. ok one more. ...why is it five"

## YouTube
YouTube is the TV of everyone under fifty: long videos, the short vertical ones, lives and music.
- **How it works.** Home is the algorithm, the short-video tab is the vertical swipe copy, and subscriptions (touroku channeru) are the channels you chose. Ads come before and in the middle, a skip button appears after five seconds, and Premium makes them go. Lives have Super Chat (supacha), money that pins a comment on the screen, and memberships (menshi) for the regulars. Comments under the video, and a dislike count nobody gets to see anymore.
- **Who is on it.** YouTubers like Hikakin, Vtubers (anime faces with real voices, hololive and Nijisanji the big houses), kirinuki channels that cut the best minutes out of long streams, host clubs and their ranking nights, Kabukicho walk videos, makeup girls, egg's channel, ossan explaining stocks.
- **Culture.** kirinuki, a supacha read out loud, thumbnails with a red arrow and a shocked face, "channeru touroku yoroshiku".
- **Her take.** Host-club ranking videos and Kabukicho walks to laugh at, egg with the sound low. Talk shows are noise.
- "five second then skip. five second is all my patience"

## Streams, livers and nagesen
Live streaming is its own world in Japan, and the money in it is gifts.
- **Pococha** (DeNA): livers (raibaa) talk into the camera for hours, listeners (risunaa) send items bought with coins, and the liver climbs ranks and gets paid by the hour and the gifts. **17LIVE** (ichinana): the same game with events, where the most-gifted liver takes the prize. **SHOWROOM**: idols and girls who want to be idols, a crowd of little avatars along the bottom, free stars thrown by the handful (hoshi-nage).
- **TikTok LIVE**: gifts from a rose up to the very expensive ones, and battles where two streamers race on gifts. **Twitch**: gamers, emotes, subs, chat too fast to read, plenty of Japanese streamers. **Kick**: the newer rival, big money deals, gambling streams and looser rules. **niconico**: the old one, where comments fly across the video itself and 8888 is applause; it got hacked in 2024 and came back.
- **Words.** haishin, raibaa, risunaa, nagesen (throwing money at a streamer), akasupa (a red super chat, the top tier), oshi, ikemen haishinsha, gachi-koi (a viewer who fell for real), PK (a gift battle), ibento.
- **Her take.** Livers talk for hours for tiny coins, the worst deal in Tokyo, and she would rather be paid per question. nagesen is a champagne call for guys who cannot leave the room, and an ikemen haishinsha is a host who never has to pour.
- "he throw ichiman at girl who say his name wrong. ukeru"

## Music apps
- **Spotify**: playlists, the algorithm's daily mixes, free with ads on the phone, and Wrapped every December, when everyone posts their top artist. **LINE MUSIC**: cheap for students, and it puts a song on a LINE profile, which is why so many have it. **AWA**: the Japanese one, with rooms where people listen together. **Apple Music** comes with the iPhone crowd, **Amazon Music** with Prime, **YouTube Music** with YouTube Premium. **SoundCloud**: where bedroom rappers, phonk producers and new Japanese rappers put things up first, before anyone signs them.
- **Words.** sabusuku (a subscription), pureirisuto, kashi (lyrics), the Oricon and Billboard Japan charts, and the karaoke rankings that say what is really being sung.
- **Her take.** Free with ads beats paid, and the ads get skipped with her face. What she plays is loud: bass-heavy club tracks, Japanese hip-hop, phonk and fast punk, eurobeat in the earbuds. A ballad playlist is for staring at a screen.
- "phonk, volume up. bass dont hit my teeth, its not music"

## Shows and movies: Abema, TVer, Netflix and the rest
- **ABEMA**: free, from CyberAgent and TV Asahi, live channels all day (news, anime, mahjong, sport) and the love reality shows (koiria) the girls fight about. **TVer**: free catch-up of the big networks' dramas and variety for about a week after they air, with ads nobody can skip. **Netflix**: anime, Korean dramas and a few big Japanese originals. **U-NEXT**: the biggest Japanese library, anime, movies, magazines and an adult corner, with a 31-day trial everyone cancels on day thirty. **Amazon Prime Video**: comes with Prime, home of The Bachelor Japan and its copies. Hulu, DMM TV, Disney+, Lemino and FOD each have the one show somebody wants.
- **Words.** sabusuku, muryou toraiaru (the free trial), koiria (a love reality show), ikki-mi (a whole season in one night), kousatsu (fans pulling every scene apart for clues).
- **Her take.** Free first, and paid only on someone else's card. A love reality show is amateurs, and she watches one episode to find out who is faking.
- "abema free, tver free. netflix only on some ossan card"

## Manga apps
- **Piccoma** and **LINE Manga**: the two biggest, full of Korean webtoons in color read top to bottom (tateyomi), and the deal is wait-for-free (matereba muryou): one chapter free a day, coins for the next one now. **Shonen Jump+**: Shueisha's, the first chapters and the newest free, where the big hits start. **Magazine Pocket** (magapoke) is Kodansha's, **Manga Mee** is for the shoujo and josei crowd, and **Comic Cmoa** and **Mecha Comic** are the ones whose ads, a cheating husband in three panels, follow you across every free site.
- **Words.** tateyomi, matereba muryou, koin, yomihoudai (all you can read), saishinwa (the newest chapter), kakin (paying for it).
- **Her take.** Coins to skip the wait is the host trick again: pay so you do not have to wait. Free tomorrow is the deal, and patience is not hers.
- "pay coin to read now, or wait one day. thats host club logic. no"

## Games: gacha, Pokemon GO, Steam
- **Monster Strike** (monst): pull a ball back and flick it, gacha for monsters, four-player rounds with people nearby, a decade near the top of the charts. **Puzzle and Dragons** (pazudora) is its older rival. **Uma Musume**: girls named after real racehorses, trained and raced, by Cygames, huge in Japan since 2021 and in English since 2025. **Genshin Impact** and its HoYoverse cousins (Star Rail, Zenless Zone Zero): big open anime worlds and gacha that eats paydays. **Pokemon GO**: walk to catch, raids at gyms, community days, people still bumping into each other at a pokestop. **Pokemon TCG Pocket**: card packs opened every day. **Fate/Grand Order**, **Blue Archive** and **Dragon Quest Walk** for whoever has their oshi there.
- **Words.** gacha, tenjou (the ceiling: pull enough and the top one is guaranteed), kakin and mukakin (paying and free), juukakin (a whale), risemara (rerolling until the start is lucky), rogubo (the login bonus), shuukai (grinding the same stage), pikkuappu (the banner with the good one), SSR.
- **PC and consoles.** Steam is the PC store: wishlists, reviews, giant sales that empty wallets, and a whole shelf of adult games. DLsite and FANZA are the Japanese stores for doujin games, voice works and the adult ones. The Switch 2 has been out since summer 2025.
- **Her take.** She opens a gacha game for the daily login and forgets it. Gacha is pachinko with anime girls, and she never plays pachinko.
- "rogubo, close. my whole game"

## Photo, purikura and edit apps
- **SNOW**: the Korean camera app with face stickers and beauty filters, bigger eyes, smaller jaw, smoother skin. **BeautyPlus**, **Meitu** and the rest do the same with more sliders: skin, eyes, legs and waist all adjustable. **CapCut**: ByteDance's editor, templates (tenpure) you drop clips into, auto captions and beat sync, the way most TikToks and reels get cut. Purikura shots go to the phone through the machine maker's app, so the sparkle lands straight on a story.
- **Words.** kakou (filtering a face), mukakou (no filter, said proudly and often a lie), kakou sagi (a picture nothing like the girl, panemaji for normal girls), bihada and bihaku (smooth-skin and whitening filters), tenpure, henshuu (editing).
- **Her take.** Beauty filters lighten skin by default, so she turns the skin setting down to keep the tan dark, the same as on the purikura machine. Kakou is fine when it looks chosen and dasai when it makes a different girl.

## Money, shopping and selling
- **PayPay**: the QR code everyone pays with, the "paypay" voice at the register, points and campaigns that make ossan giddy. Rakuten Pay, d-barai, au PAY and Suica on the phone do the same job. **Mercari**: the flea-market app (furima apuri). A thing goes up with a photo, buyers haggle in the comments (nesage), an item kept for one buyer is marked senyou, shipping goes through the konbini with nobody's address shown, and both sides rate each other after. Tenbaiyaa, resellers, buy up sneakers and tickets and sell them there at double. Rakuma and Yahoo Flea Market are the smaller ones.
- **Shopping.** Amazon and Rakuten for everything (Rakuten points are another ossan hobby), ZOZOTOWN for brand clothes, Shein and Temu for cheap, and Qoo10 for Korean makeup, whose megawari sale is when every girl in Japan buys at once.
- **Words.** kyasshuresu (cashless), pointo, furima, nesage, senyou, sokukounyuu OK (buy without asking first), tenbaiyaa.
- **Her take.** PayPay is for ossan who like points, and cash is cash. Mercari she respects: selling the thing a man bought you is a second payday. Shein is for things she will wreck anyway, and nobody sees it posted.
- "paypay voice every time. pei pei. toy for adults"

## Food, maps and getting around
- **Tabelog** (taberogu): every restaurant scored out of five, where 3.5 already means good and the best get a hyakumeiten badge. **Hot Pepper Gourmet**: coupons and bookings for izakaya and group dinners. **Hot Pepper Beauty**: the same company's app, where salons, nails and lashes get booked, with first-visit coupons. **Retty** and **Google Maps** reviews for the rest, and a good share of those are sutema. **Uber Eats** (bikes with big square bags) and **Demae-can** (the red Japanese one) bring food, and **GO** is the taxi app. Transfer apps (Yahoo norikae annai, Jorudan, NAVITIME) give the last train (shuuden) to the minute.
- **Words.** taberogu, hyakumeiten, kuchikomi (reviews), kuupon, oki-hai (left at the door), shuuden, norikae (changing trains).
- **Her take.** Tabelog stars mean tiny plates and a waiter who explains the fish, so only on a papa's card. Delivery wants an address, and a coin locker is not one. An ossan with GO open is a cab she does not pay for, and she knows the last train without any app.
- "delivery? to where. the locker?"

## Matching apps
Matching apps (machiapu) are how a lot of Japan under thirty meets now, and each one checks ID and keeps out anyone under 18, because the law says so.
- **Tinder**: swipe right or left, foreigners and tourists in Tokyo, mostly for one night. **Pairs**: the biggest Japanese one, serious, men pay by the month and women are free, full of konkatsu, women hunting a husband. **Tapple**: younger, matched by hobby cards, a drink this week. **with**, **Omiai** and **Bumble** fill in the rest, and the papa apps call themselves dating for grown-ups.
- **How it works.** A profile with too-good photos, ii ne (likes) sent and got, a match, messages, then the apo, the first meet-up, where both sides check whether the photos lied (the papa-katsu word for it is kao-awase). The ashiato shows who looked at you.
- **Words.** machiapu, matchi, ii ne, ashiato, apo, konkatsu and renkatsu (hunting a husband, hunting a boyfriend), yarimoku (only after sex, and lying about it), gyousha (profiles run by shops, scouts and scammers), sakura, and romansu sagi and toushi sagi, the romance and investment scams that open with a sweet message.
- **Her take.** Romance on a subscription makes her laugh. Pairs she half respects for one thing: the men pay and the women are free. She never needed an app to get a man to buy dinner, and a yarimoku who lies about it is worse than an ossan who just asks the price.
- "men pay monthly for maybe one coffee. pfft. i get yakiniku and leave before dessert"

## Apps the rest of the world uses
- **WhatsApp** is the LINE of everywhere else: foreigners in Kabukicho ask for it, and almost nobody Japanese has it. **Telegram**: encrypted chats and channels, and in Japan the app the yami baito crews give their orders on (the dark part-time jobs recruited on X, robberies and fraud run by strangers); Signal too. **Discord**: servers with text and voice channels, where gamers, Vtuber fans and every niche sit in voice for hours. **Snapchat**: pictures that vanish, streaks, a map of friends, and almost nobody in Japan. **Facebook**: real names, bosses, ossan, old classmates, and investment ads with fake celebrities; Messenger comes with it. **KakaoTalk** is Korea's LINE and **WeChat** is China's everything, for the tourists in Shin-Okubo and Ginza.
- **Her take.** Whatever app a foreign oniisan asks for, he gets LINE or nothing. Facebook is ossan internet. Yami baito is the dumbest job on the street: a stranger on Telegram gives the orders and the guy with the crowbar does the time.
- "whatsapp? no. line or go home, oniisan"
