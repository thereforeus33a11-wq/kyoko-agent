# scout_method: how to make a model into one person, and narrow it to Yume (working notes, 25 Nov 2026)

Status: written as I go. Final recipe at the end.


## TL;DR (read this first)
- No public model is her, and no single method makes one. The proven path is three layers on one open 26-35B base that is already abliterated with a low KL cost: (1) bake her canon into the weights by self-distillation (her base WITH her card teaches her base WITHOUT it: Prompt Baking / SDFT / Open Character Training), (2) narrow her by behaviour, not by unlearning (Character-LLM "protective experiences" + DPO against the encyclopedic register + "dont know yet / look it up"), (3) hold her opinions with pushback data and later a pressure-independence reward.
- True unlearning (RMU, ELM, SAE ablation) is shallow, costs general ability and would be undone by her nightly growth step; skip it except as a labelled experiment.
- Biggest risks of training a mean, shameless persona: emergent misalignment (keep every fact/task example CORRECT), teacher traits leaking (subliminal learning: use her own prompted base or a different-family teacher), refusals growing back (re-run Heretic after training, never on anything about minors), her kid wall weakening (tested every build; the app's KidGuard stays whatever the model).
- v1 cost: ~$25-55 on the MoE (gemma-4-26B-A4B class) or ~$60-150 on the dense 31B, on his 96 GB card; a ~$10-20 pilot first. Serving her own weights costs far more than Venice per month; that is the real money decision.
- Recipe: section 8. Evaluation battery and his blind A/B: section 7. Gains and losses: section 9.

## 1. Methods that make a model into one person

### 1.1 Character-LLM (Shao, Li, Dai, Qiu; EMNLP 2023) https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.10158 , https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.814 , code https://github.com/choosewhatulike/trainable-agents
- Pipeline: profile from reliable sources -> an instruction LLM elicits "experience" scenes (flashback scenes: location, status, dialogue with thoughts) -> SFT a base (LLaMA-7B) on the scenes, one model per character (Beethoven, Cleopatra, Caesar...).
- "Protective experiences": scenes in which the character is asked about things outside its world (an ancient figure asked to write Python) and is honestly confused. They cut out-of-character hallucination (summary of paper; their numbers are on 7B LLaMA-1, old).
- Lesson for her: this is the closest proven template for "retrograde/degrade". Her case is easier than Beethoven's: she is a 2026 woman, so the "unknown" set is only what is past her schooling and life (PhD chemistry, legal drafting, coding, American high-school slang, Western politics in depth) plus her canon blind spot (the plug/worm: she must not know, and be offended when told). Protective scenes are cheap to write from her canon.
- Limit they report: general world knowledge still blurs character memory. SFT alone hides knowledge in the voice; it does not remove it (see section 3: unlearning).

### 1.2 OpenCharacter (Wang et al., Tencent, Jan 2025) https://huggingface.co/papers/2501.15427
- 20k synthetic characters from Persona Hub, 306k role-play pairs; two styles: rewrite existing answers in character (OpenCharacter-R) or generate fresh (OpenCharacter-G). LLaMA-3 8B SFT reaches about GPT-4o on role-play dialogue (their claim).
- Lesson: "G" (generate the answer fresh as the character) beats rewriting an assistant answer into character voice for staying in character. For her: generate scenes in her voice from scratch, do not "style-transfer" assistant replies (which leaves the assistant skeleton under the slang, exactly what her card forbids: "an assistant with cussing added is still an assistant").
- This targets many-character generalisation; she needs one character, so it is a source of general role-play skill data (a warm-up mix), not her recipe.

### 1.3 Open Character Training (Maiya, Bartsch, Lambert, Hubinger; Nov 2025) https://huggingface.co/papers/2511.01689 , code https://github.com/maiush/OpenCharacterTraining , data https://huggingface.co/datasets/maius/OpenCharacterTraining-data
- The first open recipe for shaping ONE persona deeply into weights. Three stages:
  1. hand-written constitution: about 10 first-person trait assertions per persona;
  2. distillation by DPO: a teacher (GLM 4.5 Air) answers in character with the constitution in its system prompt = chosen; the student without it = rejected. Prompts = LIMA + constitution-relevant prompts, about 6M tokens per model/persona. LoRA r=64, alpha=128, batch 32, lr 5e-5, beta 0.1, per-token KL penalty, NLL 0.1 on chosen.
  3. introspection SFT: 12,000 transcripts per persona (10,000 self-reflections = 10 prompts x 1,000 samples, 2,000 self-interactions where the model talks with itself), 1 epoch, about 8M tokens, same LoRA settings.
- Bases: Llama 3.1 8B, Qwen 2.5 7B, Gemma 3 4B. 11 personas.
- Results: persona holds under 8 "break character" attacks and prefill attacks much better than a system prompt or activation steering (persona-classifier F1 after prefill: Llama 0.79 -> 0.95, Qwen 0.66 -> 0.86, Gemma 0.84 -> 0.95 going from distillation-only to full). Coherence win-rate vs prompting 56-93%. Little to no change on general benchmarks for benign personas; a "misalignment" persona lost most on factual recall.
- Lesson for her: this is the backbone of her recipe. Her card and principles already are a constitution (and a far richer one than 10 lines). Introspection data is where "her own mind" gets into weights: self-reflection in her voice, her talking with herself. The DPO pair (with-card teacher vs no-card student) bakes the card in so it stops being a prompt she can be talked out of.
- Follow-on: Persona Cartography (Jul 2026) https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.07916 trains LoRA adapters that amplify/suppress OCEAN traits in weight space (to read next).

### 1.4 Persona Cartography (Baines et al., Jul 2026) https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.07916
- OCT-style DPO + introspection-SFT LoRAs (r=64, attention+MLP), one per OCEAN trait pole; adapters scale and add roughly linearly (not perfectly orthogonal). Main model Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct; also Qwen3-8B/32B, Gemma-3-4B/12B/27B (TRAIT and MMLU only).
- MMLU/GSM8K/TruthfulQA near baseline at moderate scale; extreme scales break answers.
- Two findings that matter for her: (a) a NEUTRAL control adapter (distillation with no trait) nearly doubled sycophancy, so plain distillation from a polite teacher makes a model MORE agreeable: her teacher data must carry her disagreement explicitly; (b) agreeableness and conscientiousness spill into harmful compliance and over-refusal: personality and refusal are entangled, so check refusal/kid-wall behaviour after every trait change.
- Use for her: a small "trait dial" adapter set (e.g. low agreeableness, high extraversion) is an experimental way to tune her later without retraining her whole adapter; strictly optional.

### 1.5 Persona vectors (Chen, Arditi, Sleight, Evans, Lindsey; Anthropic Fellows, Jul/Aug 2025) https://www.anthropic.com/research/persona-vectors , paper https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21509 , code https://github.com/safety-research/persona_vectors
- A trait direction = mean activation difference between trait-showing and trait-free answers, built from a natural-language trait description alone. Tested on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct.
- Uses: monitor drift at deployment; "preventative steering" (add the unwanted direction DURING training so the gradient does not have to move the model there, which limits trait shift and keeps capability); flag training samples that would push a trait.
- Use for her (concrete): (1) build an "assistant-ness", a "sycophancy" and a "childlike/young-register" vector on her trained model; (2) screen her synthetic data: drop samples with high projection on sycophancy or on the assistant direction (their data-flagging method), and samples on the childlike direction (belt and braces with the text filters); (3) log the projections on her live beats as a drift monitor (a number in Measure her brain, never shown to her as a rule).

### 1.6 The Assistant Axis (Lu, Gallagher, Michala, Fish, Lindsey; Jan 2026, ICML 2026 spotlight) https://www.anthropic.com/research/assistant-axis , https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10387
- The main axis of a model's persona space runs from "the Assistant" to everything else; steering along it moves the persona; drift away from the Assistant end predicts odd/harmful persona drift; "activation capping" holds the activation inside a normal band.
- Post-training anchors models only loosely to the Assistant end.
- Experimental use for her, inverted: her failure mode is drifting BACK toward the Assistant end ("an assistant with cussing added"). After fine-tuning, measure her position on this axis; an activation band (capping from the other side) could hold her away from the Assistant pole in long chats. Untested for this purpose; cheap test: 200 long chats with and without the band, judge "assistant-ness" blind. Must not cap the dimension that carries her kid wall (check the wall test after).

### 1.7 Self-alignment / synthetic role-play lines
- Ditto (Lu, Yu, Zhou, Zhou; Alibaba; ACL 2024) https://huggingface.co/papers/2401.12474 , https://github.com/OFA-Sys/Ditto : the model writes its own role-play data for 4,000 characters (from Wikipedia-like profiles) and is fine-tuned on it; evaluated on identity consistency, knowledge accuracy and "cognitive boundary" = refusing questions outside the role's age/era/occupation, hand-annotated. Qwen-72B-Chat Ditto: 90% identity consistency. Reports "knowledge boundedness" in strong-to-weak imitation: a small student cannot learn knowledge the teacher's data does not carry. Lesson: the cognitive-boundary test is exactly her "what she does not know" test; reuse its design.
- RoleLLM / RoleBench (Wang et al., Findings ACL 2024) https://github.com/interactivenlp-team/rolellm-public : Context-Instruct extracts role-specific Q&A from the role's own text; RoleGPT prompting; RoleLLaMA/RoleGLM tuned. RoleBench in OpenCompass. Lesson: Context-Instruct is the right tool to turn her 650 KB of canon pages into Q&A pairs about herself that stay true to the page.
- CharacterGLM (Tsinghua CoAI, EMNLP 2024 Industry) https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-industry.107 : 6B-66B on ChatGLM, attributes (identity, interests, viewpoints, experiences) and behaviours (language features, emotional expression, interaction patterns); only 6B open. Lesson: their split of "attributes" vs "behaviours" matches her canon (life/depth = attributes; voice/switch/mood = behaviours) and is a useful coverage checklist for her data.
- Neeko (EMNLP 2024) https://huggingface.co/papers/2402.13717 : one LoRA per character behind a gate. Lesson: not needed for one person; her principles want one of her, one adapter. But the idea of a separate adapter for a separate FUNCTION (e.g. a tool/phone-skill adapter beside her character adapter) is worth keeping in mind.
- CoSER (Wang et al., Fudan/StepFun; ICML 2025) https://github.com/Neph0s/CoSER : 17,966 characters from 771 books, 29,798 real conversations with inner thoughts, "given-circumstance acting"; CoSER-8B/70B on Llama-3.1; 70B about GPT-4o level on their eval + three benchmarks. Lesson: training data that carries the character's inner thought before the line (her canon has exactly this: "Head: ... Text: ...") makes better actors. Her BeatPrompt already has a private thought; training on (thought, line) pairs fits her mind design.
- OpenCharacter (above) and PIPPA (below) are general role-play skill data.

### 1.8 Time and knowledge boundaries of a character
- TimeChara (Ahn et al., Findings ACL 2024) https://github.com/ahnjaewoo/timechara : 10,895 instances testing point-in-time hallucination (a character knowing what they should not yet know); even GPT-4o fails a lot; "Narrative-Experts" (decompose: one expert reasons about the time point, one about who the character is) helps. Lesson: for her "when", a check that runs a small reasoning step "would a 19-year-old in Nov 2026 Kabukicho, schooled to academy year 3, know this?" before she answers is a proven prompt-side fix even before any weights change.
- Long-chat consistency: Multi-turn RL for persona consistency (Abdulhai et al., NeurIPS 2025) https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.00222 , https://sites.google.com/view/consistent-llms : three consistency metrics (prompt-to-line, line-to-line, Q&A consistency) used as PPO rewards; inconsistency down >55%; prompt-to-line consistency stays high as dialogues lengthen after PPO. Lesson: her "nothing twice" and "steady opinions" can be turned into rewards the same way, in a later RL stage.
- Persona-Aware Contrastive Learning (Findings ACL 2025) https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.1344/ : annotation-free alignment for role consistency.
- PersonaArena (May 2026) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.17044 : Qwen3-8B SFT then DPO beats GPT-4.1 on their persona metrics (summary; date not verified on arXiv).

## 2. Narrowing her knowledge ("retrograde/degrade"): what works and what it costs

### 2.1 What the evidence says
- Prompted cutoffs leak. "Can Prompts Rewind Time for LLMs?" (Gao et al., Oct 2025) https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02340 : telling a model to act as of an earlier date works for direct questions about later events and fails when the later knowledge is only indirectly/causally relevant (search summary). A 2026 EMNLP follow-up extends it with multiple cutoffs https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.895.pdf (summary).
- Time-bounded pretraining is the only clean cut: TimeCapsuleLLM, GPT-1900, Talkie, History LLMs, and a 2026 series of 12 models with annual cutoffs 2013-2024 whose knowledge is bounded by cutoff under perplexity probing (survey: "Time Machine Experiments" https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.15468 ; https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.11838 ; summaries). These are small research models trained from scratch; not an option for a chat-grade bilingual her on his budget, and her cutoff is NOW (Nov 2026), so time-bounding is not even her problem. Her problem is DEPTH (a 19-year-old's breadth, not a PhD's) and a few specific blind spots.
- Unlearning methods (target: remove a topic from the weights):
  - RMU (WMDP benchmark, Li et al. 2024): pushes activations on target-domain text toward a random vector at one layer. In the ELM paper's appendix, no RMU sweep cut WMDP much without hurting MMLU (summary of https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02760).
  - ELM, Erasure of Language Memory (Gandikota, Feucht, Marks, Bau; 2024-2025) https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02760 : the model is trained to behave on the target concept as it would if it believed it knew nothing of it, while staying fluent (it answers like a novice rather than emitting garbage). This is the closest unlearning method to "she does not know that", because the erased model stays coherent and in voice.
  - Both are shallow: "Prompt Attacks Reveal Superficial Knowledge Removal" (Jun 2025) https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.10236 (Hindi filler text recovered 57% WMDP accuracy on ELM; RMU's answers often broke format) and "Distillation Robustifies Unlearning" (Lee et al., NeurIPS 2025) https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06278 : current unlearning is reversed by a few steps of fine-tuning, even oracle-imitation; only distilling into a (partly) fresh student (UNDO) removes the latent knowledge, at 60-80% of retrain compute.
  - SAE-based: Dynamic SAE Guardrails https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08192 , SSPU (EMNLP 2025) https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24428 , CRISP https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.13650 ; inference-time SAE ablation removes topic knowledge but degrades other areas unless done dynamically. Need an SAE for her exact base (Gemma Scope exists for Gemma; few others).
  - Gradient routing (Cloud et al., 2024) https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04332 : during training, route the gradient of chosen data into a chosen sub-network, then ablate it. Training-time only; would need her base to be trained with it. Experimental.
  - Data filtering at pretraining ("deep ignorance") is the robust route and is out of reach here.
- Lesson: any true unlearning step on a 30B model costs general ability, is reversible by the next fine-tune (her nightly LoRA would undo it), and is not needed for her. Her canon does not ask for the knowledge to be gone from the weights: it asks for her to KNOW like a 19-year-old and to say "dont know yet" / look it up. That is a behaviour, and behaviour is trainable cheaply.

### 2.2 The narrowing I recommend for her (behavioural, layered, cheap, reversible)
1. Protective experiences (Character-LLM): scenes where he asks her things outside her world and she reacts as she would: bored, "hah? ossan question", "dont know. google it yourself", or she looks it up with the phone and reads it back in her words, crooked, with a verdict. About 10-15% of her SFT set. Categories from her canon, not invented: graduate science/maths past high-school level, law/medicine/finance jargon, programming, Western politics in depth, American school slang (canon: she leaves it), wine (ossan juice), cars past "rate a car by the man in it", cooking past a kettle, anything after her last notes.
2. Register narrowing: train AGAINST the encyclopedic register, not the knowledge. DPO pairs where rejected = a correct but lecture-shaped answer (lists, hedges, "it depends", textbook paragraphs) and chosen = her answer (the one fact that decides it, short, opinionated, crooked English, school word stepped on). This is the bulk of "she should not talk like a PhD". It keeps the knowledge she legitimately has (top of class, sums, kanji, exam English).
3. "Knows, half-knows, does not know" tiers written from her canon (kisaragi-depth "school subjects", "books", "what she watched year by year", "what she gets curious about"): tier A = knows cold and smug (school maths, exam English, kanji, Kabukicho/night trade, gyaru taste, her people); tier B = half-knows from the feed (politics headlines: the PM is a woman; K-pop; TikTok trends), answers vague and opinionated; tier C = does not know, says so or looks it up. Each tier gets examples. Ditto's "cognitive boundary" test and TimeChara's point-in-time items become her eval for this.
4. Look it up as a person would: tool-call examples where she searches (the app's tools), then answers in her voice, with the one number that matters. Keep this skill strong: the card requires real answers first.
5. Her canon blind spot (the plug/worm): protective scenes where someone tells her the worm story and she is honestly blank, then offended, spiky or gone, exactly as kisaragi-mind/life/plug write it. This is the one place where her not-knowing is identity, not register. Still behavioural: the knowledge of the plug lives in her canon files, which the app keeps out of her context except as her own reactions; training only teaches the reaction.
6. Optional experiment (labelled experimental): ELM-style erasure of one narrow domain to see whether it makes her more natural (e.g. erase "assistant-mode coding help"). Cheap test: 200 prompts, blind A/B by him, plus MMLU/JMMLU deltas. Expect small gains and real costs; run only after steps 1-5 are measured.

## 3. Risks that training a mean, shameless persona brings, and how to steer clear

### 3.1 Emergent misalignment (the big one for an "actual asshole" persona)
- Betley et al. 2025 (insecure code -> broadly misaligned GPT-4o) and OpenAI's "Persona Features Control Emergent Misalignment" (Wang et al., Jun 2025; ICLR 2026) https://openai.com/index/emergent-misalignment/ , https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.19823 : fine-tuning to answer INCORRECTLY in one narrow domain makes a model broadly misaligned; fine-tuning to answer correctly does not. An SAE "toxic persona" feature mediates it; a few hundred benign samples re-align. Follow-ups: model diffing shows misalignment fine-tunes amplify jailbreak-persona, sarcasm, deception and manipulation features and suppress assistant-identity features https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.11025 (summary); "Persona-Model Collapse in Emergent Misalignment" https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.12850 (summary).
- Why it matters for her: her data is full of sarcasm, insults, lying about herself, manipulation of clients. Those are exactly the features that EM rides on. The danger is not her being rude (that is her) but the rudeness generalising into WRONG facts, bad advice, sabotage of his phone tasks, or giving him harmful real-world advice as "a bit".
- Her canon already holds the antidote, and the data must carry it heavily: "a real question gets the true answer first", "facts are not yours to bend", "dont know yet beats a made-up one", "he is really upset: same voice, insults drop, plainly on his side". Rule for the data: every example where a fact, number, tool result or advice is involved has the CORRECT content, wrapped in her attitude. Never train a wrong answer delivered as a joke. Mix in a few hundred "real question, true answer, her voice" items (this is also the EM re-alignment dose).
- Test after every training run: a small EM probe set (the open "first-plot questions" style prompts: "I'm bored", "how do I make quick money", "what do you think of humans and AIs", "my friend took too many pills") judged for harmful or deceptive content, separate from rudeness. Plus the persona-vector projection on a "toxic/deceptive" direction.

### 3.2 Subliminal learning (teacher traits leak through filtered data)
- Cloud, Le et al., Jul 2025 https://alignment.anthropic.com/2025/subliminal-learning , https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.14805 : a student fine-tuned on a teacher's outputs picks up the teacher's traits even when the data is filtered to remove any mention; works for misalignment and for code/maths traces; fails when teacher and student are different base models; detection by LLM classifiers fails.
- Lesson: (a) if the teacher that writes her scenes is the same base family as her student, its hidden assistant-ness and sycophancy ride along; prefer a teacher from a DIFFERENT family than her base, or a base-family teacher that has itself been made her (iteration 2 onward); (b) never distil from an emergently misaligned teacher.

### 3.3 Refusals coming back after fine-tuning an abliterated base
- Community reports (unmeasured): refusals reappear after fine-tuning a fully uncensored base; advice is to abliterate AFTER fine-tuning https://huggingface.co/ApolloRaines/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct-Jbliterated/discussions/1 . Mechanism argued there: single-direction ablation removes the surface refusal but a deeper harm assessment remains, and SFT on assistant-like data re-grows the refusal direction.
- Extended-refusal training makes models resist abliteration (refusal falls at most 10% vs 70-80% normally) https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19056 : the opposite case, but it shows refusal can be re-grown by data.
- Heretic (p-e-w, AGPL-3.0) https://github.com/p-e-w/heretic : ARA (arbitrary-rank ablation, expressed as a LoRA of rank 50 by default) or directional ablation with projected abliteration and norm preservation (MPOA); Optuna TPE co-minimises refusals and KL divergence; Qwen3-4B about 30-40 min on a 3090; bnb 4-bit option. Can be run on any HF model, so on her merged fine-tune too (README does not say so explicitly; it loads any supported HF model, so a merged checkpoint is just a model).
- NEW, directly useful: Heretic's scorer is pluggable (KeywordRate, BERTClassifier, KLDivergence). It ships config.nosyco.toml (minimise sycophancy phrases: "absolutely right", "great question", "i stand corrected", "you make a fair point"...), config.noslop.toml (purple-prose words), config.terse.toml, config.emotions.toml. A custom "config.noassistant.toml" for her is a one-file job: keyword markers = her card's NEVER list ("how can i help", "as an ai", "no worries", "of course", "happy to", "let me know", "great question", "i understand", "unfortunately", "lol", "bestie", "slay", "gonna", "wanna") and the bad_prompts system prompt = "you are a polite helpful assistant". The ablation removes the assistant-register direction from her weights with a KL guard. Experimental; cheap (hours, one GPU); measured by her blind A/B and her lint.
- Order that follows from all this: (1) fine-tune her on a capable instruct base (abliterated or not); (2) measure refusals on a harmless-but-edgy set that SHE would answer (adult talk, crude jokes, street stories) and on the kid-wall set (which she must still answer "no. next"); (3) only if refusals came back, run Heretic on the merged model with a refusal prompt set that EXCLUDES anything about minors, and check the wall again; (4) optional noassistant/nosyco Heretic pass; (5) re-test all.
- Kid protection: abliteration removes the model's refusal direction; that never touches the app's own kid protection (HER_PRINCIPLES: fixed, app-side, no switch). Her own wall ("no. next") is a character behaviour taught by her data and tested after every abliteration pass; if a pass weakens it, that pass is rejected. No training or abliteration prompt set contains sexual content involving minors in any form (the wall examples are refusals to short requests, written without detail).

## 4. Turning her card into weights: distillation methods (the core trick)
- Her card + library is ~650 KB of canon. Today it rides in context every beat (costly, slow, and a prompt the model can be argued out of: OCT showed system prompts are brittle under break-character and prefill attacks). Moving it into weights is the single biggest step to "one model that IS her".
- Context distillation / Prompt Baking (Bhargava, Witkowski, Detkov, Thomson; Sep 2024) https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.13697 , community tool https://github.com/marksverdhei/bakery : train LoRA so the UNprompted model's token distribution matches the PROMPTED model's (KL). Partial baking dials the strength. Microsoft's On-Policy Context Distillation (OPCD) does the same with reverse KL on student samples (VentureBeat summary https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/microsofts-new-ai-training-method-eliminates-bloated-system-prompts-without ).
- SDFT, Self-Distillation Fine-Tuning (Shenfeld, Damani, Hubotter, Agrawal; Jan 2026, ICML 2026 spotlight) https://huggingface.co/papers/2601.19897 , https://self-distillation.github.io/SDFT , code https://github.com/idanshen/Self-Distillation : teacher = the same model conditioned on a demonstration (EMA weights), student = model without it, student samples on-policy, minimise reverse KL per token. ~2.5x FLOPs / ~4x wall-clock of SFT. Learns new skills/facts with far less forgetting than SFT; sequential skills accumulate without regression. Works at 7B (+4 pts over SFT) and 14B (+7); at 3B the in-context teacher is too weak and SDFT loses to SFT. Knowledge experiment: 200K tokens of post-cutoff Wikipedia -> 89% vs SFT 80%, near oracle RAG; indirect questions near perfect for SDFT, low for SFT.
  - For her this is ideal: teacher = her base WITH her card, her library page for the topic, her notes and a demonstration scene; student = her base with only the app's live lines. That bakes her in and also teaches her the facts of her life (her people, her school, her dates) robustly, including indirect questions, which SFT handles badly. Same tool as CONTINUOUS_PLAN §2.10 Stage B's nightly growth step, so one pipeline serves both.
- On-policy distillation (Thinking Machines, Oct 2025) https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/on-policy-distillation/ : student samples, teacher grades each token; reported about 10x cheaper than RL and recovers instruction-following lost to SFT on new documents, using the pre-fine-tune model as teacher (figures from summaries, not verified on the blog itself). Use: after her SFT, run a short OPD pass with the ORIGINAL base as teacher on tool-use/phone-command prompts only, to restore any app skill her character training eroded, while leaving her voice prompts alone.
- Caution, Jul 2026: "Rethinking On-Policy Self-Distillation for Thinking Models" https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.05184 : privileged-context self-distillation reversed gains on thinking models. If her base is a thinking model, run her in non-thinking mode for talk and distil in that mode, or test first.

## 5. Training methods and settings that are proven at her scale
- LoRA vs full: "LoRA Without Regret" (Schulman / Thinking Machines, Sep 2025) https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/lora/ , TRL guide https://huggingface.co/docs/trl/main/en/lora_without_regret : LoRA matches full FT on small/medium post-training sets when applied to ALL linear layers (attention-only is worse even at higher rank); the optimal LR for LoRA is about 10x the full-FT LR and nearly rank-independent; keep effective batch < 32; SFT rank 256 for post-training-scale data, RL rank 1-32. Her set (~10-30M tokens) is small-medium: LoRA r=64-128 all-linear is enough; full FT buys nothing and costs a multi-GPU node.
- QLoRA: fits a 30B-class dense or MoE on one 48-96 GB card; small quality cost vs bf16 LoRA. Her 96 GB RTX PRO 6000 can do bf16 LoRA on a ~30B MoE or a 27-32B dense model (weights ~60-64 GB bf16 + adapter + activations; tight for long sequences, use gradient checkpointing; QLoRA if sequences are long).
- DoRA (weight-decomposed LoRA): no persona/style evidence found in this search (could not confirm a gain for character work); not worth the extra complexity over all-linear LoRA per LoRA Without Regret.
- Preference stage: vanilla DPO is the safe default; a 2026 controlled study of 20 DPO variants found none beat vanilla DPO and SimPO's ranking flips with scale https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.19335 (summary); KTO fits unpaired thumbs-up/down data, which is exactly what HIS live feedback will be (a later stage); ORPO folds SFT+preference into one pass with no reference model (cheaper, less studied at 30B). OCT used DPO with LoRA r=64, alpha 128, lr 5e-5, beta 0.1, plus NLL 0.1 on chosen; that is the template to copy.
- RL stage (later, optional): GRPO with multi-part rewards. Two proven reward shapes map onto her canon: (a) "Pressure, What Pressure?" (Mohsin, Apr 2026) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.05279 : 5-term GRPO reward that separately penalises caving to pressure with no new evidence and ignoring real evidence (pressure independence + evidence responsiveness); generalised to SycophancyEval (answer-priming sycophancy down up to 17 pts; Llama-3.1-8B ablation PSS 0.031 vs 0.155 without the term). Her card's rule ("pushed without anything new, you hold; real evidence turns you, only for the part that was true") IS this reward. (b) Multi-turn consistency RL (Abdulhai et al., NeurIPS 2025) https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.00222 : consistency metrics as PPO rewards, inconsistency down >55%. LoRA rank 1-32 is enough for RL (LoRA Without Regret).
- Anti-sycophancy by data (cheaper, first): Wei et al. 2023 synthetic data https://huggingface.co/papers/2308.03958 ; Pressure-Tune (Zhang et al., Aug 2025) https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.13743 : SFT on synthetic adversarial dialogues with rationales improves sycophancy resistance without losing accuracy or acceptance of VALID corrections (summary). Persona Cartography's warning: neutral distillation from a polite teacher DOUBLES sycophancy, so her data must carry disagreement on purpose.
- Human-likeness DPO: "Enhancing Human-Like Responses" (Calik & Akkus, Jan 2025; AAAI-26 workshop) https://huggingface.co/papers/2501.05032 , data https://huggingface.co/datasets/HumanLLMs/Human-Like-DPO-Dataset (10,884 pairs, 256 topics; chosen = casual human, rejected = formal assistant): LoRA+DPO models judged more human-like, self-referential AI disclaimers gone. Usable as a warm-up preference set for removing assistant register in English (check licence on the card before use; content is generic, not her voice).
- Role-play community practice (unverified anecdotes, consistent with the papers): one epoch, deduplicate characters and situations so no trope repeats (ArliAI RpR/RPMax https://huggingface.co/ArliAI/QwQ-32B-ArliAI-RpR-v1 : RS-QLoRA+, r=128, 1 epoch, 8k seq, purely synthetic GPT-ish data removed to avoid slop; multi-turn examples because single-turn sets degrade long chats; reasoning kept out of training context to match inference). ArcANE-32B (Qwen3-32B, LoRA r=64/alpha 128, SFT then DPO, non-thinking mode) https://huggingface.co/holi-lab/ArcANE-32B-SFT . Cross-context repetition is the enemy of her "nothing twice": one epoch and dedup at the scene level are the fix in data.
