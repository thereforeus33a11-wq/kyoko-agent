# What life added to Yume: for you to strike

Date: 24 Nov 2026. For her owner.

The research looked at real women of exactly her kind in Japan: their slang, the law around them, their bodies, their nights. What it found was held against her canon. Two of her pages grew from it:
- `kisaragi-depth/SKILL.md`: now 8,561 words, up from 7,939.
- `kisaragi-body/SKILL.md`: now 4,850 words, up from 3,064.

The versions before this pass are kept beside them as `SKILL.md.before_kind`.

What holds across the whole pass:
- Five critics read the drafts: canon, Japan as it is, her as a person, her principles, and her own eyes. Their fixes are applied.
- Nothing in her canon was changed. Where life and her canon differ, her canon stayed on the page, and the difference is listed at the end of this file for you.
- Nothing added goes near her ring, her stomach or the night she cannot reach.
- Nothing makes her younger.
- School appears only as a grown woman remembering it.
- Nothing new is said about you.

Each line below is one addition. Strike any you do not want, and it comes out of the page.

## Where her canon may be wrong: yours to judge

These are lines in her canon that life shows differently. Builders changed none of them. Her past stays as it happened; a dated price or a picture of the street is your call. The confidence is the research's own.

1. **ukeru** may now read as a 2010s word. Low confidence: one weak dictionary page.
2. **dopagaki** is SHIBUYA109 lab.'s top SNS meme of 2026, not an egg magazine word. High.
3. **"joto kaiwai" winning egg's 2026 slang vote:** no 2026 award was found. egg's 2025 award was announced on 29 Nov 2025, so the 2026 one is probably not out yet. Medium.
4. **Her height.** Medium.
   - 158 cm is about average for Japanese women in their 20s, so she looks up at men and tall women, not "nearly everyone".
   - In her platforms she is near the average man's eye line.
5. **Her ichiman** is low against Tokyo's papa-katsu going rate of about 30,000. Low confidence: affiliate sites.
6. **Host billboards and rankings.** A June 2025 police notice and an October 2025 industry rule banned "No.1" and sales rankings from ads, and many Kabukicho billboards were blacked out. Laughing at the rankings and hair-guy's billboard may be out of date. High.
7. **Urikake.** Kabukicho host clubs scrapped urikake, the pay-later tab, from April 2024. Momo's rising "tab" would now be card debt, a consumer loan, loan-shark money or someone fronting it. High.
8. **The manga cafe night pack** costs about 2,860 yen in Kabukicho now, not about 1,500. Medium.
9. **Smoking rooms.** High.
   - Since April 2020 nobody under 20 may enter any smoking room.
   - A paper-cigarette karaoke smoking room may not serve drinks, so a nomihoudai smoking room is not a legal room.
   - Her "hatachi" may cover her getting in.
10. **Master's back room.** In Tokyo only a bar its owner runs alone may allow smoking, and it must post that under-20s may not enter. So master would be breaking that rule for her. High.
11. **taspo** was announced to end in March 2026. If it did, the card no longer exists. Low confidence; check first.
12. **Her first drag.** First cigarettes usually bring a head rush and nausea, not calm. Her past does not change, so this is for you only. High.
13. **Her red hair at the academy.** A strict private girls' academy would more likely have made her dye it back to black than pin it flat. Medium.
14. **Sega's "cherrit" purikura** could not be verified. Furyu makes almost every machine. Medium.
15. **"-be" on the end of her angry Japanese.** That belongs to northern and western Saitama. Kawaguchi speaks near-standard Tokyo Japanese. It can stay as her quirk, but it is not typical. Medium.
16. **A smaller check: her ramen shop's name.** The research names a 24-hour Hakata ramen shop in Kabukicho "Wazo" (和蔵). If it is the same shop as her "Wakura", canon reads the name differently.

The critic who checked Japan as it is found no fact in the new lines that contradicts her canon, and no anachronism.
